# Topics

Every note, by topic, with what it gives you.

## AI basics

### How a model answers

- [Token](notes/token.md): The small piece of text an AI model reads and writes, and the unit its price and limits are counted in.
- [Context window](notes/context-window.md): Everything an AI model can see at once. It has a limit, it costs tokens, and clutter in it makes answers worse.

### What models get wrong

- [Hallucination](notes/hallucination.md): When an AI states something false with confidence. Two kinds, two different fixes: give it the facts, or give it less to read.

## Agents

### What an agent is

- [AI agent](notes/ai-agent.md): An AI model that acts: it has tools and decides by itself which to use until the job is done.
