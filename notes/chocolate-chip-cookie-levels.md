# Chocolate chip cookies from scratch

_Six from-scratch chocolate chip cookie recipes, each built on the one before: upgraded Toll House, aged, loaded, Levain-style and a toasted-flour finale._

One baking creator made ten levels of chocolate chip cookie ([Binging with Babish, 0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=51s)). This keeps levels 5 to 10, the from-scratch ones, with every amount they say. Why a lever works is in What controls a cookie's texture. "Amount not said" means the video gives none.

## Level 5: upgraded Toll House

Level 4 is the original Toll House recipe, the standard chocolate chip cookie ([Binging with Babish, 8:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=494s)). Level 5 upgrades it ([Binging with Babish, 10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=636s)). This is a half batch ([Binging with Babish, 12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=741s)).

- Butter, ½ cup, browned in a small pan for 5 to 10 minutes ([Binging with Babish, 10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=636s)).
- 100 g all-purpose flour and 40 g bread flour. Baking soda and kosher salt: the amount is unclear in the captions ([Binging with Babish, 12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=741s)).
- 75 g white sugar. Dark brown sugar: amount not said ([Binging with Babish, 12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=741s)).
- About half of one extra egg yolk. The whole egg: amount not said. ½ tsp vanilla ([Binging with Babish, 13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=821s)).
- Chocolate chips, less than a cup (amount not said), plus a semisweet bar. Chop some in chunks and some into shavings ([Binging with Babish, 13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=821s)).

## Levels 6 to 8: aged, loaded, costly

**Level 6, aged.** Keep the level 5 dough 3 days in the fridge before baking ([Binging with Babish, 16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=974s), [16:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=993s)). One night also shows ([Binging with Babish, 17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1058s)).

**Level 7, kitchen sink.** Add to the aged dough ([Binging with Babish, 18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1135s)):

- Walnuts, lightly toasted in the oven, then chopped.
- About 1 tbsp very finely ground hazelnut coffee. Grind whole beans espresso-fine, as ready-ground is coarse and speckles the cookie.
- Heath pieces, pretzels, kettle chips, caramel corn and Cinnamon Toast Crunch. Amounts not said.

Stir in the crunchy extras after the dough has aged, or they absorb moisture and go soft ([Binging with Babish, 20:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1223s)).

**Level 8, expensive ingredients.** The Toll House recipe with costly ingredients ([Binging with Babish, 22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1362s)). The creator's lesson is that dearer ingredients did not make a better cookie ([Binging with Babish, 25:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1539s)). See "Not price" in What controls a cookie's texture.

## Level 9: Levain-style copycat

A copy of a New York bakery's cookie, from a colleague's recipe: a big ball, crisp outside and soft inside ([Binging with Babish, 26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1608s)).

1. Cut 1 cup cold butter into small cubes. Cream it (beat it smooth) very slowly with 400 g white sugar, so it stays cold ([Binging with Babish, 26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1608s), [28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1700s)).
2. Add 2 cold eggs, one at a time, then 2 tsp vanilla paste ([Binging with Babish, 28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1700s)).
3. Add 180 g all-purpose flour, 173 g cake flour, 2 tsp kosher salt and 1½ tsp cornstarch. The video names no baking soda or powder ([Binging with Babish, 28:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1700s)).
4. Fold in 1 cup semisweet chocolate, 1 cup dark chocolate and 1½ cups walnuts ([Binging with Babish, 29:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1773s)).
5. Make 170 g balls and chill them 1 hour ([Binging with Babish, 29:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1773s)).
6. Bake at 400°F for about 10 minutes. The browning was uneven, so the creator tried 375 to 380°F on convection, which browned more evenly ([Binging with Babish, 29:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1773s)).

The centre stays raw on purpose ([Binging with Babish, 29:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1773s)).

## Level 10: the final version

The first try stayed puffy. It used buckwheat flour, 250 g dark brown and 100 g white sugar, 1 tbsp salt, 1 tbsp coffee and 2 eggs ([Binging with Babish, 33:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1985s), [33:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2036s), [38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2336s)). The second try below is the one the creator calls level 10 ([Binging with Babish, 37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2239s), [45:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2705s)).

**Day 1: the parts**

1. Toast 170 g all-purpose and 90 g bread flour on a rimmed tray at 350°F for about 25 minutes, stirring every 5. Cool it fully ([Binging with Babish, 31:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1877s), [32:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1924s)). Sift it, or specks of flour show ([Binging with Babish, 38:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2287s), [38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2336s)).
2. Caramelize (slowly brown) white chocolate at 275°F for about 45 minutes, stirring every 15. If it turns clunky, use a food processor. Chill it solid, then chop ([Binging with Babish, 32:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1924s), [33:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1985s)).
3. Brown the butter and chill it solid. Amount not said ([Binging with Babish, 33:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1985s)).

**The dough**

4. Beat the butter with 175 g white and 200 g dark brown sugar, and 2 tsp vanilla paste. Add 2 eggs and 1 extra yolk ([Binging with Babish, 33:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=1985s), [37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2239s), [38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2336s)).
5. Mix in the toasted flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 2 tsp kosher salt and 1 tsp finely ground hazelnut coffee ([Binging with Babish, 37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2239s), [38:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2287s)). Use spelt flour where the first try had 50 g buckwheat ([Binging with Babish, 33:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2036s), [37:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2239s)). Mix gently: the creator guesses the first dough had too much gluten ([Binging with Babish, 38:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2336s)).
6. Add 15 oz chocolate: 5 oz semisweet chips, 5 oz chopped dark and 5 oz caramelized white ([Binging with Babish, 38:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2287s)).
7. Chill overnight ([Binging with Babish, 40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2401s)).

**Bake**

8. If the dough is in scooped balls, leave them out 15 minutes. Break each open and press it back together, so the dry outside does not stop the spread ([Binging with Babish, 41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2468s)).
9. Bake at 330 to 350°F for 14 minutes: 6, rotate, 6, then take it out and bang the tray on the counter a few times. Bake 2 minutes more ([Binging with Babish, 40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2401s), [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2468s)).
10. Sprinkle flaky salt on top. Cool at least 10 minutes on the tray ([Binging with Babish, 42:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dzw6JO-yz34&t=2551s)).

**Careful:** The oven, trays and browned butter are very hot: use oven gloves, and do not leave butter browning unwatched.

## My recipe (Level 6)

How I make them, for 30 cookies.

| Practice | What I do |
|---|---|
| Butter | 227 g unsalted, browned every time, with ice |
| Flour | All-purpose and bread flour, 225 g in all |
| Sugar | 200 g white and 120 g dark brown |
| Eggs | 2 eggs and 1 extra yolk |
| The rest | 1 tsp each of vanilla, baking soda and salt |
| Chocolate | 1 pack of chips plus a 100 g bar, chopped |
| Rest | Overnight in the fridge, then at least 3 days in the freezer |
| Bake | 175°C for 14 minutes |
