# Claude Opus

_Anthropic's family of models for heavy, open-ended work: long reports and hard code. It sits between Sonnet and Fable._

Claude Opus is a family of AI models made by Anthropic ([Chase AI, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=0s)). A family keeps one name while new versions of it come
out. Each version is a release, with its own note.

## What it is for

- **Thinking a job through.** Anthropic says to start with Opus while you are still working out what you want, then
  move to Sonnet once you know ([Claude, 1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9WfoNW2XnE&t=107s)). In its own example, Opus does the
  report and Sonnet the quick edits to the slides after it ([Claude, 0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9WfoNW2XnE&t=51s)).
- **Where the big gains came.** Anthropic says its clearest recent gains in [agents](ai-agent.md), models that work through a task
  step by step on their own, were in the Opus class ([Theo - t3․gg, 4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSV-7ywHxeU&t=241s)).
- **Often cheaper per job than it looks.** Its price per word is higher than Sonnet's, but the bill can be lower:
  see Claude Sonnet.

## What it is not

- **Not Anthropic's strongest family any more.** That is Fable, which Anthropic calls its most
  powerful model class ([Anthropic, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uVS88gnaxcg&t=0s)).
- **Not replaced by Fable.** Fable is a class of its own, not the next Opus. Opus 5 came out in July,
  after Fable 5 in June, and both lines went on ([Fireship, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxGJT1weu4w&t=0s)).
- **Not a separate product per app.** Opus is a model. Claude's chat app and [Claude Code](claude-code.md) run the same
  one underneath, one reviewer points out ([Nate Herk | AI Automation, 3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0WDkwMxj13s&t=183s)).

## What changed

- 2026-09-22 · big: Opus 5.5 is the newest Opus.
  Was: Opus 5, whose writing one review calls full of jargon ([Chase AI, 3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=233s)).
  Now: [Opus 5.5](claude-opus-5-5.md), which Anthropic says writes more plainly ([Chase AI, 3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=233s)) and comes near
  Fable 5.1 for 40% less ([Chase AI, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=0s)).
- 2026-07-29 · big: Opus 5 is the newest Opus.
  Was: Opus 4.8. Now: Opus 5, sold as near Fable for half the price
  ([Fireship, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxGJT1weu4w&t=0s)). Testers found it made up more answers than Opus 4.8 did ([Fireship, 1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxGJT1weu4w&t=79s)).
