# Claude Opus 5.5

_Said to come close to Fable 5.1 for less money, and to write more plainly than Opus 5._

Claude Opus 5.5 is the [Opus](claude-opus.md) release that came out on 22 September 2026. A review
published that day calls it "a brand new Opus model" ([Chase AI, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=0s)).

## What changed from Opus 5

It follows Opus 5 ([Chase AI, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=0s)).

- **Near Fable, for less.** Anthropic claims it does about as well as Fable 5.1 at 40% lower
  cost, one review reports ([Chase AI, 0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=0s)).
- **Better at code.** The same review sees large gains over Opus 5 on almost every coding test
  ([Chase AI, 0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=24s)).
- **Cheaper per [token](token.md).** $4 per million tokens in and $20 out, below Opus 5's price ([Chase AI, 2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=148s)).
  Fable 5.1 costs $10 in and $50 out ([Chase AI, 3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=220s)).
- **Against Opus 5.** Higgsfield, a video tool that offers the model, says it is 40% cheaper and over 30% faster than Opus 5 ([Higgsfield AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9loDDILgssM)).
- **Plainer writing.** Anthropic says 5.5 fixes how Opus 5 wrote: it uses less jargon and fewer odd phrases, and
  keeps to the writing rules you give it ([Chase AI, 3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=233s)).
- **Fable's guards.** Anthropic says it treats risky topics, such as cybersecurity and biology, almost the way Fable
  does. Questions it will not take go to Opus 4.8 instead ([Chase AI, 4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=274s)).
- **A demo with bricks.** Anthropic's demo has Opus 5.5 design a brick model from a description or a photo. The model comes with a full step-by-step manual. The app's engine checks every connection: the 2,874-piece model takes 487 steps, and none of its 15,880 stud joints is flagged as weak ([Claude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCR9epzSNGc)).
- **Behind Duet.** A KodeKloud video says Claude Duet is based on Opus 5.5 ([KodeKloud, 0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rhdlfTJFOH4&t=47s)).
- **Middle Effort is enough.** On one coding test, medium effort, meaning how long the model thinks before it
  answers, scored as well as maximum for a sixth or seventh of the cost ([Chase AI, 2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ijPsFZ1EHlI&t=148s)).
- **Anthropic's own pitch.** Its model list says Opus 5.5 leads in agentic coding and knowledge work, and costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads ([Anthropic, Introducing Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/models#:~:text=Introducing%20Claude%20Opus%205.5)).
- **It can over-build.** Told to keep improving a website for 100 rounds, it grew the site to 21,000 lines ([Nate Herk | AI Automation, 12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DByAR5Su1Yw&t=724s)). See Make it better, 100 times.
- **Prompts that suit it.** Anthropic's guide says to name what "done" looks like and to delete "think carefully" lines, as it thinks before every reply: Prompting Opus 5.5 ([Anthropic, Try this first](https://claude.dev/blog/getting-the-most-out-of-opus-5-5#:~:text=Try%20this%20first)).
